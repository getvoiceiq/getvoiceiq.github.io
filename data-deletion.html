Delete your data

Last updated: 12 October 2026

You can delete your VoiceIQ data at any time. Deletion is permanent and completed within 30 days. Removed data leaves our backups within 7 days after that.

Delete your whole account

  1. Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my VoiceIQ account”.
  2. We confirm within 7 days and delete your account, analyses, comments, comparisons, poll summaries and connected-account keys within 30 days.

We keep only payment records that Kenyan tax law requires us to keep.

Disconnect a platform

In VoiceIQ, go to Account → Connected accounts → Disconnect. The access key is deleted straight away. Comments already imported stay in your analyses until you delete them or your account.

Facebook and Instagram

  1. On Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations.
  2. Find VoiceIQ and choose Remove. Tick the option to delete posts and activity if offered.
  3. Meta notifies us automatically. We delete your Facebook and Instagram access keys and the data imported from them within 30 days, and give you a confirmation code you can use to check the status of your request.

YouTube (Google)

Remove VoiceIQ at Google security settings, then email us to delete imported data.

TikTok and LinkedIn

Remove VoiceIQ in your TikTok or LinkedIn account settings, then email us to delete imported data.

If you are a person whose comment was analysed

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] with a link to or copy of your comment. We will remove it from our systems where we hold it, and pass your request to the account that analysed it where required.

Questions or complaints

Contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL]. You can also contact the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.
