3. How we use it, and our legal basis

  • To provide VoiceIQ (contract): analyses, reports, emails about your account and analyses.
  • To keep it secure and working (legitimate interest): preventing abuse, fixing errors, preventing duplicate free trials.
  • To send news and offers (consent): only if you tick “Send me VoiceIQ news”. You can untick it at any time.
  • To send our newsletter (consent): only if you subscribe using the form on our website and agree to receive it. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to remove you. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything already sent.
  • To meet legal duties (legal obligation): tax records and lawful requests.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, build profiles of individual commenters, or use it to train AI models. For public figures, our reports never tie criticism or political opinions to a named person.

6. Connected platforms

You may connect accounts you manage so VoiceIQ can import comments for analysis. We ask only for read access, use it only to produce your analyses, never post or reply on your behalf, and show the data only to you and your team.

Facebook and Instagram (Meta)

With your permission through Facebook Login for Business, we read the list of Pages you manage, your Page posts and the comments and ratings on them, and, for a linked Instagram professional account, your media and its comments. You can remove VoiceIQ at any time in Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations. When you do, Meta tells us and we delete your access keys and imported data as described on our data deletion page.

YouTube (Google)

VoiceIQ uses YouTube API Services to read comments on videos you choose. By using this feature you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke VoiceIQ’s access at Google security settings. VoiceIQ’s use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

TikTok

If you connect a TikTok business account, we read your videos’ basic details and the comments on them, only to produce your analyses. You can disconnect in VoiceIQ or remove access in your TikTok account settings.

LinkedIn

If you connect a LinkedIn Page you administer, we read its posts and their comments. LinkedIn data from a Page is shown only to that Page’s administrators in VoiceIQ. You can remove access in your LinkedIn settings or disconnect in VoiceIQ.

7. Comments from other people

Comments you analyse may contain names and words of people who commented publicly. We process them only on your instructions and only to produce your analysis. If you are one of those people and want something removed, contact us and we will help, and pass your request to the account that analysed it where required.

8. Your rights

Under Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019 you can ask to be informed, access, correct, delete, object to processing, and receive a copy of your data. Email [SUPPORT EMAIL]; we reply within 7 days and complete requests within 30 days. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited by strict database rules so each account sees only its own data, access keys for connected platforms are never sent to your browser, administrators use two-step sign-in, and every sensitive action is logged. If a breach affects your data, we will tell the ODPC within 72 hours and inform you without delay where required.

10. Children

VoiceIQ is for organisations and adults. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

11. Cookies and storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores your sign-in session and a few settings (such as names you track in Mentions) so the service works.

We will post any change here with a new date, and email you about important changes.
